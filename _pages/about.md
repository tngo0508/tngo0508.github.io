## What I Write About

This blog is where I learn in public. I write in simple English for beginners and for anyone who wants a clear, step-by-step explanation. Most posts cover:

- **C# and .NET**, including ASP.NET Core and EF Core
- **Azure, DevOps, and CI/CD**, such as Docker and GitHub Actions pipelines
- **Software architecture** and design
- **LeetCode and algorithms**, with worked solutions

I also write about my own projects. For example, my [Building a Personal Finance App]({% post_url 2026-10-22-personal-finance-app-series-overview %}) series follows a .NET 10 app from an empty solution to a deployment on Azure.

If a post helps you, or you spot a mistake, I'd love to hear from you.
